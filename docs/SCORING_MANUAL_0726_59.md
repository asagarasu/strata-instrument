## 0. The walk (the unit story — her 札札弄机杼 law)
A line comes in.
1. **Every unit is measured.** The line's projection on every axis, and every
   token's deletion-Δ. Measurement is never gated.
2. **Triggered tokens are investigated.** A token triggers when **|Δ| ≥ its
   field's cut — TWO-SIDED** (her ruling 07-28: both poles are salient;
   coherent with the cuts' own |Δ| derivation).
   **[AMENDED — census v5.0, 2026-07-28, her ruling ("'re-census' please"; commit
   66d673b + registration trigger_law_salience_positive_registration_0728_62.md):
   the three SALIENCE fields {colour, sound, plant} now trigger on the POSITIVE
   side only (`Δ ≥ cut`) — the salience axes read DOMAIN ENGAGEMENT, so a negative
   Δ is DILUTION, never a salience event (silence probe, silence_probe_diag_62).
   The two VALUE rulers (illumination dark+, temporal-duration long+) remain
   TWO-SIDED (`|Δ| ≥ cut`). Cut VALUES are unchanged; the two-sided text above is
   kept as the pre-v5.0 record.]**
   Only triggered tokens are
   probed — descriptive? latent-written? latent-referent? — and attribution
   never migrates between words. **A triggered token no channel claims is
   GHOST** (her definition, 07-28, verbatim: *"labse thinks this word in the
   sentence (in situ) is giving a very high scalar score, and by our
   validation this has exceeded the tok-cut … we check if it is descriptive,
   latent-written or latent-referent. And sometimes we find nothing. It is
   unexplanably strong."*). The token-ghost is the ONLY ghost that makes
   states.
3. **Findings fold upward — ONCE.** Token findings fold to LINE states.
   The poem fold is RETIRED (07-27, her word: "illogical to fold").
4. **A verdict is a crossing of LINE states** — source line × its aligned
   seat line(s). **Alignment files are LAW** (PI-approved, chair-verified,
   corpus/alignments/): a count-mismatched seat folds by its content-faithful
   map (precedence fold across the mapped lines); a DROPPED source line
   (seat []) reads as absent — the deformation the mapping itself found;
   translator ADDITIONS (unmapped seat lines) are declared, never crossed.
   A seat with no file carries an honest status.
The token is the unit of investigation; the line is the unit of state and of
verdict. No grain speaks for another.
