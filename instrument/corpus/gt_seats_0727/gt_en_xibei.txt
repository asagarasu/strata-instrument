A towering building stands in the northwest,
Its heights level with the drifting clouds.
Latticed windows feature intricate, patterned carvings,
And the pavilion rises in three-tiered terraces.
From above come the sounds of strings and song—
How deeply sorrowful the melody rings!
Who could have composed such a tune?
Surely, it must be the wife of Qi Liang.
The *Qingshang* notes rise upon the breeze,
Lingering and wavering in the mid-melody.
With every strum come repeated sighs,
A stirring passion steeped in lingering grief.
It is not the singer's toil that brings such pain,
But the scarcity of those who truly understand the music.
Oh, to become a pair of soaring swans,
And beat our wings to take flight on high.
