## Notes on structure

- Pages p224–p286 (63 files, printed pp. 208–270) were read in full sequence; every page contained appendix text (no blank or purely section-header pages).
- No entry numbers are missing; the appendix runs continuously from Nr. 1 to Nr. 100, with four places where the source itself assigns one heading to a multi-part poem/cycle spanning more than one sequence number (Nr. 30/31, Nr. 33/34, Nr. 41–44, Nr. 59/60).
- The bulk of entries (Nr. 1–18) are excerpts from the *Buch der Lieder* (Shijing); Nr. 19 onward move chronologically through Chinese literary history (Warring States/Han through Qing), ending with Qing-dynasty poets Cheng Pan-ch'iao and Li Ching-kai.
- "Poet / Attribution" reflects exactly what Debon prints under each entry (personal name with dates, "Anonym" with an approximate date, or a named dynasty/period for poets without exact dates).
