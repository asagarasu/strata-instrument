## Notes

- **Front-matter roman-numeral folios are largely inferred, not directly read.** Only "vii" (Foreword) is textually sourced, from the Contents listing on p011.pdf. No roman-numeral glyph was independently confirmed printed on p009.pdf or elsewhere in front matter. Treat the p006–p014 page-file assignments in the Part A notes as file-order fact but their roman-numeral positions (iv–xii) as unconfirmed inference.
- **"Wang Wei, 10-12"** in the Index (p246.pdf, printed page 232) is transcribed exactly as it renders in the scan/OCR. This looks anomalous — Wang Wei's chapter runs printed pages 169-78, and "10-12" does not correspond to any obvious Wang Wei content near the Introduction — but no clearer reading was available from the scan, so it is preserved verbatim rather than silently corrected. Possible original misprint or an OCR misread of "169-72" or similar; flagged for anyone consulting this index directly against the physical/scanned book.
- **No blank/unreadable/garbled pages were encountered** in either the front-matter range (p001–p015) or the back-matter range (p238–p252) beyond ordinary intentionally-blank leaves (p002, p004, p010, p012, p014, p242, p247–p251), which are noted as blank rather than treated as errors.
- **Part B is a general index, not a poem/title/first-line index.** The book's ~200 poems are presented untitled (identified by poet and occasional descriptive tag within running text) rather than by discrete titles, so no title- or first-line-keyed back index exists to extract; the "INDEX" (p243–p246, printed pp. 229-232) indexes poets' names, prosodic/genre terms, and thematic topics instead. This is stated up front in the Part B section above rather than forced into a poem-title table format.
- p252.pdf is the printed back cover (dust-jacket-style promotional summary of the book, author bio, ISBN), included for completeness in the file range scanned but not part of the book's internal back matter.
