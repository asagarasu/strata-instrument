# Die chinesische Anthologie, Band II (von Zach 1958)

- **Title:** Die chinesische Anthologie. Übersetzungen aus dem Wen hsüan. Band II.
- **Edition:** Harvard-Yenching Institute Studies XVIII, Harvard University Press, Cambridge Mass., 1958, ed. Ilse Martin Fang, introd. James Robert Hightower. Von Zach 1872–1942. © The Harvard-Yenching Institute. Distributed in Great Britain by Oxford University Press.
- **TOC source files:** No standalone Inhaltsverzeichnis exists in Band II. Front matter is minimal: `pages/vonzach_band2_524p/p001.pdf`–`p010.pdf` (blank leaf; "University of California Medical Center Library" ownership stamp; blank leaves; half-title "Volume II" + Internet Archive digitization notice; Harvard-Yenching title page; copyright page) — then the text runs straight into "XXXIV. BUCH" on `p011.pdf`. The structure below was therefore reconstructed from the in-text Buch/genre/author/piece headings running through `p011.pdf`–`p496.pdf`, cross-checked throughout against the running heads (author + Buch-numeral + piece-number printed at the top of almost every page) and, at the back, against the "Verzeichnis der Veröffentlichungen" (`p507.pdf`–`p509.pdf`, a list of prior journal publications of individual pieces), the "Autoren Verzeichnis" (cumulative author index for the whole 2-volume work, `p515.pdf`–`p518.pdf`), and "Die Teile des Wen-hsüan nach dem Inhaltsverzeichnis des Liu-ch'en chu Wen-hsüan" (`p511.pdf`–`p513.pdf`, a genre-concordance table, badly OCR'd). `p519.pdf`–`p524.pdf` are blank end leaves / library due-date stamp.
- **Printed ↔ file offset:** **printed page = file page + 596** (i.e. `p011.pdf` = printed p. 607, continuing Band I's pagination). Verified by direct visual inspection of the page-PDF images (not just the OCR layer):
  - `p011.pdf` (start of "XXXIV. BUCH") shows "607" typeset at the foot of the page → 11 + 596 = 607. ✓
  - `p430.pdf` (start of "AUS DEM LX. BUCH") shows "1026" at the foot of the page → 430 + 596 = 1026. ✓
  - Additionally cross-checked against the continuous run of OCR'd folio numbers for file pages 11–518 (formula holds for every page checked except one: the OCR text layer at file p456 reads "1032" where the formula predicts "1052" — a likely OCR digit-confusion (5↔3), not independently re-verified against the page image; it is an isolated single-page discrepancy in the text layer and does not affect the offset formula elsewhere).
- **Buch range of this volume:** XXXIV–LX (Wen xuan juan 34–60) — **with a confirmed internal gap**: Bücher LII, LIII, and LIV (juan 52–54) are **not present** in this volume. The text runs directly from "AUS DEM LI. BUCH — ABHANDLUNGEN, ERSTER TEIL" to "LV. BUCH — ABHANDLUNGEN, FÜNFTER TEIL" (i.e. Teil 1 → Teil 5, skipping Teile 2–4), with continuous printed pagination (939 → 956) and no Buch heading, genre heading, or running-head occurrence for LII/LIII/LIV anywhere in the volume — this rules out a scanning defect and confirms a genuine editorial omission (von Zach apparently did not translate/include these three Bücher of Abhandlungen). One apparently contradictory data point was checked and resolved: the back-matter Autoren Verzeichnis has an entry "Yüan Yü … *LII 1", but this is an OCR misreading of "XLII 1" (the leading X was lost) — Yüan Yü's piece 1 is correctly located under Buch XLII in the body (see table). In addition, an unnumbered-by-Wen-hsüan appendix (file pp. 449–496/499ff.) supplies translations from **Yü Hsin's own separate collected works** (not the Wen hsüan), organized under *his own* book divisions "AUS DEM I./II./III./IV./VI. BUCH" (again skipping "V"). These carry the same "AUS DEM [N]. BUCH" label as the main Wen-hsüan Bücher and could easily be mistaken for Wen-hsüan Buch I/III/IV/VI (which belong to Band I) — they are kept in a clearly separated final section of the table below to avoid that trap.
