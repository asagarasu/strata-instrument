## Findings against open questions

1. **青青河畔草: ABSENT from the 1902 edition** (as from 1867 and from
   Hervey 1862). No lady-at-window / green-grass / willow / singing-girl
   candidate under any title; the Han-era additions are Shijing items,
   Wudi's 秋風辭, and the Tisseuse céleste below. → See
   `ensemble/qingqing_hepancao/giles_en_1898.md` for what this negative
   proves about the Heilmann crib chain (S8).
2. **"La Tisseuse céleste" (p. 87, 無名氏) is NOT 迢迢牽牛星.** Read in
   full (canvases f125–f126; Weaver-girl plate at f128): first-person
   Weaver monologue — west/east separation, "Fleuve d'Argent" (footnoted
   La Voie lactée), the Bouvier "mon amant" footnoted "une étoile du
   Capricorne," one-night-a-year reunion, and a consoling close ("il sait
   bien que nous avons à nous toute l'éternité") that matches 鵲橋仙-family
   legend material (兩情若是久長時 inverted into consolation), not the
   gushi's weeping-shuttle scene (no 札札, no 泣涕如雨, no 脈脈不得語).
   No new 迢迢 seat here — recorded to save the trip.
