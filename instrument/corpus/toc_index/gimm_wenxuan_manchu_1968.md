Fuchs (Cologne) — see note under Table 2. Filed under the 2026-07-19 delivery that was hunting von Zach Band I; this is not that book, but it is directly useful transmission-history material for establishing which Wen xuan pieces existed in a Manchu translation and where.

### B. Kölner Handschrift — 6 Wen-xuan-overlapping pieces only

**Important structural note, directly stated in Gimm's Einführung (p. 15):** the "Kölner Handschrift" is *not* a second Wen-xuan translation. It is a separate Manchu manuscript (4 small draft booklets labelled *yüan/heng/li/chen* 元亨利貞, undated, style suggests early–mid 18th century, extant copy dated 1890) containing roughly 60 Chinese guwen-prose pieces from the Zhou through Song periods (philosophers, *Shih-chi*, *Tso-chuan*, etc.) — an idiosyncratic miscellany whose index booklet mislabels it "Ku-wen yüan-chien" 古文淵鑑, but Gimm establishes it is **not** the same text as the famous 64-chüan imperial *Ku-wen yüan-chien* (1685/86 preface), which had its own, separately published Manchu translation. Of this ~60-piece miscellany, only 6 pieces happen to also be Wen xuan pieces; those 6 are what Gimm transcribes here, identified via Shiba Rokurō's *Monzen sakuin* (Wen xuan concordance) running numbers. Each piece below was individually verified against its `[roman numeral, folio]` chüan-locator in the running text (all 6 ✓).

## Target verdicts

**Coverage established:** The Leningrad Hs (Hefte I–X) exhaustively covers Wen xuan chüan 1–19 — i.e., the entire *fu* 賦 genre and nothing else (confirmed structurally: 10/10 Heft-opening chüan brackets read directly, see Table 2). The Kölner Hs contributes 6 further, unrelated prose pieces at chüan 33, 41, 43, 44, 45, and 58 (卜居, 書, 移, 檄, 對問, 碑文 genres). **No part of either manuscript, as catalogued in this volume, touches Wen xuan chüan 20–32** — the entire 詩 (shi/poetry) block, which includes 樂府 (yüeh-fu, chüan 27) and 雜詩 (za-shih, chüan 29) — is absent. This is a clean structural gap, not a close call: piece 59 (the last fu, chüan 19) is followed directly by piece 316 (chüan 33) with nothing in between.

- **飲馬長城窟行 (Yin-ma ch'ang-ch'eng-k'u hsing, Wen xuan Buch/chüan 27, 樂府, 古辭/anonymous):** **NOT FOUND.** Chüan 27 falls inside the uncovered chüan 20–32 gap. (The task's flagged trap — a Ch'en Lin 陳琳 attribution circulating for this yüeh-fu outside the Wen xuan — is moot here either way: neither the anonymous Wen xuan version nor any Ch'en Lin variant appears anywhere in this Manchu translation as catalogued.)
- **古詩十九首 (Ku-shih shih-chiu shou, Wen xuan Buch/chüan 29), including #2 青青河畔草 (Ch'ing-ch'ing ho-pan ts'ao), #5 西北有高樓 (Hsi-pei yu kao-lou), #10 迢迢牽牛星 (T'iao-t'iao ch'ien-niu hsing):** **NOT FOUND.** Chüan 29 likewise falls inside the uncovered chüan 20–32 gap. None of the 19 gushi, nor any other Wen xuan shih poem, is translated in either manuscript as catalogued in this volume.

No page citations are given for either target because there is nothing to cite: the coverage gap is total across all Wen xuan poetry (chüan 19cont.–31), not merely these two items.
