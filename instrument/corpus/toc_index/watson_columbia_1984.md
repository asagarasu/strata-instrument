## Printed-page ↔ file-page mapping (two-page-spread scans)

Each page-file (`pXXX.pdf`) is a scanned two-page spread: a left (verso) printed page and a right (recto) printed page rendered as a single image, each numbered independently in the printed book.

Front matter (unnumbered / roman-numeral pages) does not follow a confirmed arithmetic pattern and was not fully resolved (see Notes). The main sequence, however, follows a clean linear relationship, verified at nine independent sample points spanning the entire book (file pages 011, 026, 037, 065, 076, 102, 121, 138, 151, 169, 180, 190, 192, 195):

> For file page **F ≥ 5** (once arabic pagination is running): left printed page = **2F − 8**, right printed page = **2F − 7**.
>
> Equivalently, for printed page **P ≥ 2**: file page **F = (P + 8) / 2** if P is even (P is the left/verso page of that file), or **F = (P + 7) / 2** if P is odd (P is the right/recto page of that file).

## Notes

- **This TOC does not enumerate individual poems.** The printed Contents page (p003.pdf) lists only front matter, twelve chapters, and back matter. Poet names appear in the TOC only where the chapter title itself names poets (Chapters Five, Seven, Eight, Ten); individual poem titles (e.g., "On the River," "Song of the Breaking of the Willow," seen while sampling chapter-opening pages) are listed only within the body of each chapter, not in the front-matter contents. If a full poem-by-poem index is needed, it would require paging through each chapter individually — out of scope for this front-matter TOC extraction.
- **Printed page number of the Contents page itself (p003.pdf) is not visible/legible in the scan.** No roman-numeral folio was captured in the transcription for p001.pdf (title/half-title), p002.pdf (copyright page / series editorial-board page), or p003.pdf (Contents). Based on standard front-matter layout and the fact that "Principal Dynasties of Chinese History" is explicitly printed as page **x** in the Contents listing, the Contents page itself is presumably an unnumbered or low roman-numeral recto page (e.g., vii) preceding it, but this was not directly confirmed from the image.
- **Chapter Four entry (printed page 93, file p050)** was derived from the verified linear formula but was not independently re-opened/re-read to visually confirm the chapter-heading text, unlike all other chapters, which were spot-checked directly. Given the formula held exactly at nine other independent sample points across the full span of the book (pp. 15 through 383), high confidence is warranted, but this one entry is flagged as formula-derived rather than directly visually confirmed.
- **p199.pdf**, read during verification, falls after the Index of Poets (383) and contains unnumbered back-matter advertising pages for other Columbia University Press series ("Modern Asian Literature Series," "Introduction to Oriental Civilizations," "Neo-Confucian Studies," etc.) — not part of the book's own Contents and not included in the table above.
- No pages encountered during this extraction were blank, unreadable, or garbled.
