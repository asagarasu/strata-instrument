## Notes

1. **File-order anomaly in the English CONTENTS pages.** Filenames p020–p025 are NOT in true reading order. By content: p020 lists entries 1–11 (pp.3–21), p021 lists entries 12–28 (pp.23–69), **p024** lists entries 29–47 (pp.73–111), and p022 lists only the final two entries 48–49 (pp.113, 115). So the true reading order is p020 → p021 → **p024** → p022. Interleaved in the filenames between these are: **p023**, a half-title/section page reading "汉魏六朝诗 / Golden Treasury of Chinese Poetry in Han, Wei and Six Dynasties" (no TOC content, no visible page number), and **p025**, a blank page. I verified the total entry count on both sides (49 = 49) and the roman-numeral sub-numbering (其二↔II, 其三↔III, 其九↔IX, etc.) to confirm this reordering — every one of the 49 rows lines up cleanly zh↔en once p024 is moved between p021 and p022. This is likely a binding/scanning artifact in the physical book or its PDF export, not an error on my part in reading the pages — flagging clearly since anyone re-deriving page-files from filenames alone would get it wrong.

2. **Offset correction.** The assumed offset `file = printed + 22` is off by one; the correct offset is **`file = printed + 23`**, confirmed via two independent content pages (p026 = printed p.3, footer "·3·", contains 大风歌; p139 = printed p.116, footer "·116·", the book's final page, tail end of 梅花落/"The Mume Blossoms"). Printed page numbers do not appear to be visible/legible on the TOC or title pages themselves (p017–p025) — they're unnumbered front matter — so this offset was established from content pages, not the TOC pages directly.

3. **zh vs en printed-page divergence.** Starting at entry 5 (琴歌二首, zh p.7 vs en p.8) the printed page numbers listed in the two TOCs drift apart, growing to a 4-page gap by entry 28 (定情诗/"A Woman's Love", zh p.65 vs en p.69), before both TOCs converge again at the very last entry (zh p.115 = en p.115). This is consistent with the book's layout: Chinese and English text for a given poem do not always begin on the identical physical page (a longer poem's Chinese text may fill a page while its English translation spills onto the next), so each TOC's page number reflects where that language's text starts, not a single shared page number. I have not independently confirmed this against every content page — flagging as inference, not verified fact, in case it matters for downstream indexing.

4. **Possible OCR/romanization uncertainties**, transcribed verbatim as printed/rendered rather than "corrected": "Po Qin" for 繁钦 (entry 28 — a plausible reading given 繁 has a rare surname pronunciation "Pó", but worth spot-checking against the actual poem page); "Xu Wo" for 徐幹 (entry 37 — expected modern pinyin would be "Xu Gan"); "Xia Lang" for 谢朗 (entry 45 — expected "Xie Lang", parallel to "Xie An"/"Xie Daoyun" in the same credit line). None of these affect the zh↔en pairing, which was confirmed positionally and via the numeral markers.

5. **"Anoymous"** (entries 16–20, all attributed to 无名氏) is printed thus in the source English CONTENTS pages (missing the "n" in "Anonymous") — transcribed verbatim as a likely printing-house typo, not corrected.

6. All pages read (p017, p018, p019, p020, p021, p022, p024, and the adjacency-check pages p001, p016, p023, p025, p026, p139) were legible; none were blank/garbled except p025, which is intentionally blank (not an error).

7. No entries beyond the 49 listed were found — p019 (last zh TOC page) and p022 (last en CONTENTS page) both trail off into blank space after entry 49, and content-page footer numbering (p139 = printed 116) is consistent with the book ending shortly after 梅花落.
