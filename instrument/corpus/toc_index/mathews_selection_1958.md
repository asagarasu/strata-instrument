# The Flowers of Evil (Mathews selection — New Directions NDP-71, 1958)

- **Title:** The Flowers of Evil (A Selection)
- **Edition:** Edited by Marthiel and Jackson Mathews, New Directions paperbook NDP-71, 1958 (selection drawn from the 1955 complete edition; multiple translators)
- **TOC source files:** `<HOME>/garden/books/dnd2027/corpus/pages/The flowers of evil/p011.pdf` – `p012.pdf` (Contents, pages ix–x); Index of Translators: `p187.pdf` – `p188.pdf` (printed pages 167–168).
- **Printed ↔ file offset:** arabic printed page N = file p(N+20). Verified: printed 63 (the two Spleen poems) = p083; printed 167/168 (Index of Translators) = p187/p188. Roman front matter: roman N = file p(N+2) (Contents ix = p011).
- **Translator credits:** the Contents has no translator column; per-poem translators are given in the "Index of Translators" (printed pp. 167–168) and signed under individual poems in the body. The translator column below is filled in from that index. The two "Spleen" poems share printed page 63 and the index lists "Spleen 63" under both Kenneth O. Hanson and Sir John Squire; the body page (file p083) shows "Old Pluvius" signed "— Kenneth O. Hanson", so "When the low heavy sky" is Squire's.
- "The Unforseen" is spelled thus (sic) in both the Contents and the Index.
