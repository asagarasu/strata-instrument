# TEXTUAL VARIANT (final line), DECLARED, not silently normalized:
#   The standard 中華書局/逯欽立 text and most modern anthologies print
#       欲辨已忘言   (辨 biàn 'to discern / tell apart')
#   維基文庫 prints
#       欲辯已忘言   (辯 biàn 'to argue / dispute')
#   辨/辯 are a well-attested interchange in the transmission of this line; both

#   The variant does not touch the target crossing (L7) and is illumination-inert.

# The 10 lines (五言, one 5-character verse per line), text of record:

結廬在人境，
而無車馬喧。
問君何能爾？
心遠地自偏。
採菊東籬下，
悠然見南山。
山氣日夕佳，
飛鳥相與還。
此中有真意，
欲辨已忘言。
