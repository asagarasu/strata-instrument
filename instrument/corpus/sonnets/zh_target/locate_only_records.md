## 1. 梁宗岱 Liang Zongdai (1903-1983)

Translated ALL 154 sonnets. History: began translating in the late
1930s; completed all 154 by the early 1960s; the complete set was
serialized in the Hong Kong 《文汇报》 supplement May 1963 - March
1964; after the Cultural Revolution he re-translated/revised the
whole set in a few months (1976-77); the revised complete set was
first published in book form in 《莎士比亚全集》第十一卷, 人民文学出版社,
1978 — the last of his works published in his lifetime.

Editions to excerpt from (verified in retail/library listings):
- 《梁宗岱译集：莎士比亚十四行诗》, 上海: 华东师范大学出版社, 2016-08,
  hardcover, 332 pp., ISBN 9787567544994.
- 《莎士比亚十四行诗》(巴别塔诗典系列, 精装), 北京: 人民文学出版社,
  2020-01, ISBN 9787020153848.
- 《莎士比亚全集》第十一卷, 北京: 人民文学出版社, 1978 (first book
  publication of the revised translation).

## 2. 梁实秋 Liang Shiqiu (1903-1987)

Translated the COMPLETE works including all 154 sonnets — the first
person to complete a solo Chinese translation of the complete
Shakespeare (1930-1968, 37 years; prose renderings). First edition:
《莎士比亚全集》, 台北: 遠東圖書公司 (Far East Book Co.) — plays
published 1967, the full 40-volume set completed 1968; the sonnets
volume is part of the 1968 completion. Rights note: per a
translators.com.cn profile, Liang sold the complete-works rights
outright to Far East Book Co., which still holds the 梁译本 copyright
(https://www.translators.com.cn/archives/2007/03/952).

Editions to excerpt from:
- 《莎士比亚全集》(40 vols.), 台北: 遠東圖書公司, 1967-1968 — sonnets
  in the poems volume (pre-ISBN era; no ISBN exists for the first
  edition). NOT FOUND: a specific ISBN for the current Far East
  sonnets volume — Far East's own catalog listing was not reachable
  in this session; mark for human follow-up.
- Later mainland licensed reprint line: 《莎士比亚全集》(中英对照,
  40 册), listed on Douban at https://book.douban.com/subject/1190913/
  (中国广播电视出版社/远东图书公司 co-edition; the sonnets are one of
  the 40 volumes).

## 3. 屠岸 Tu An (1923-2017)

Translated ALL 154 sonnets — the FIRST complete standalone Chinese
translation of the Sonnets. Publication history (via multiple
concordant press profiles):
- First edition: 《莎士比亚十四行诗集》, 上海: 文化工作社, 1950-10
  (列入"译文丛书"; first complete Chinese single-volume Sonnets, with
  Tu the PI's per-poem 译解 commentary).
- Revised: 1963 revision (unpublished then); 《十四行诗集》, 上海译文
  出版社, 1981-05 (over 500 revisions; Fang Ping 方平 involved
  editorially) — Douban record https://m.douban.com/book/subject/3231632/.
- Further revised editions: 重庆出版社 (with Penguin), 2008-03;
  additional revisions around 2016; his translation has appeared from
  ~10 publishers, incl. 《莎士比亚十四行诗(精)/屠岸译文集》, 北方文艺
  出版社, 2019, ISBN 9787531744504; e-book 《莎士比亚诗歌全编：十四行诗》
  (得到/人民文学出版社 line).
- Obituary/history summaries: https://www.yicai.com/news/5006239.html ,
  https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_1909448 ,
