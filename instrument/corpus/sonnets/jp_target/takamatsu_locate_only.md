## 高松雄一 Takamatsu Yuichi (1929-07-25 - 2017-08-19)

English-literature scholar and translator; professor at the
University of Tokyo (emeritus 1990), later Komazawa University; died
of interstitial pneumonia at 88 (Wikipedia
https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%AB%98%E6%9D%BE%E9%9B%84%E4%B8%80 ;
obituary https://bookpooh.com/archives/3351 ). Known for the Iwanami
Bunko complete Sonnets, the co-translation of Joyce's Ulysses (with
Maruya Saiichi et al.), and Durrell's Alexandria Quartet.
