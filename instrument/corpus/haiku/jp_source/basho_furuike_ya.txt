NOTE ON TEXTUAL VARIANTS (checked, not assumed):
  (a) Orthography: this 1903 edition prints 「古池や蛙とびこむ水の音」
      — とびこむ in kana. The most common modern printed form is
      蛙飛びこむ (also seen: 飛び込む / 飛こむ). Same word, same
      reading; orthographic variation only, but noted because exact-
      string searches against this file must use とびこむ.
  (b) Draft/variant history (from ja.wikipedia article
      「古池や蛙飛びこむ水の音」, consulted 2026-07-05):
      - An earlier form 「古池や蛙飛ンだる水の音」 (tobindaru, a
        colloquial Danrin-style contraction) appears in 『庵桜』
        (Iozakura, early 1686), regarded as the initial draft.
      - Bashō's disciple 其角 (Kikaku) proposed 山吹や ("yamabuki ya",
        kerria roses) for the opening five; Bashō rejected it. A
        transmitted alternative text 「山吹や蛙飛び込む水の音」
        appears in the 1700 collection 『曠山集』-tradition sources.
      - First publication: 『蛙合』 (Kawazu awase), 3rd month of 1686,
        where it is the opening verse; reprinted in 『春の日』 (Haru no
        Hi, one of the 俳諧七部集) in the 8th month of the same year.
        The 春の日 text is the canonical modern form.
  (c) Cross-references within this corpus: en_target/ holds two PD
      English translations (Aston 1899, Chamberlain 1902); zh_target/
      holds Zhou Zuoren's 1923 Chinese rendering. Blyth and Chen
      Li/Zhang Fenling versions are in-copyright, locate-only — see
      ../manifest.md.

蛙

古池や蛙とびこむ水の音
蛙子は目すり鱠をなく音哉
哀れさは鳶の巢になく蛙哉
