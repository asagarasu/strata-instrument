NOTE ON TEXTUAL VARIANTS (checked, not assumed):
  (a) Within Oku no Hosomichi manuscript copies, this edition's
      apparatus records for the hokku itself: main text しみ込 with the
      note 「一本しみ入るトアリ」 (= "one copy reads しみ入る"). The
      form famous today, and the one used for this cluster's target
      transliteration (shimiiru), is the しみ入る reading; this base
      text happens to print the しみ込 (shimikomu) reading as its main
      text. Two further apparatus entries in the same passage: 開基にして
      (一本しノ字ナシ) and 勝閑 (一本淸簡トアリ). Note also that this
      edition prints 勝閑 where most modern texts print 清閑.
  (b) Composition history (from ja.wikipedia 「閑さや岩にしみ入る蝉の声」
      and related pages, consulted 2026-07-05): Bashō's travel companion
      河合曾良's notebook (俳諧書留) records the initial on-site draft as
      「山寺や石にしみつく蝉の声」; an intermediate transmitted form
      「さびしさや岩にしみ込む蝉の声」 is also reported. The final Oku
      no Hosomichi text is the 閑さや… form. So the poem has a genuine
      three-stage revision history, and the しみ込/しみ入る manuscript
      split above is on top of that.
  (c) Second attestation within this corpus: the 1903 『芭蕉俳句全集』
      page saved as basho_furuike_ya_raw.html (same jp_source/ dir)
      also prints this hokku — in its 夏/蟬 section, under the headnote
      立石亭 (sic; where Oku no Hosomichi has 立石寺) — reading
      「閑さや岩にしみ入るせみの聲」, i.e. with the しみ入る form as its
      main text. So the two Wikisource-hosted editions in this corpus
      split exactly along the しみ込/しみ入る manuscript division noted
      in (a).
  (d) Cross-references within this corpus: no PD English translation of
      this hokku was found in Aston 1899 or Chamberlain 1902 (both were
      full-text searched — see ../manifest.md, Gaps). Blyth's version is
      in-copyright, locate-only.
