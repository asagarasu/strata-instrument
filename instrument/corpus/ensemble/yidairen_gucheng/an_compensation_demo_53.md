# 顾城《一代人》— the 30-second compensation demo (the PI, 2026-07-21)
**Source (one line, whole poem):** 黑夜給了我黑色的眼睛，我卻用它尋找光明

**Canonical English (widely circulated):** "The dark night gave me
black eyes / I use them nonetheless seeking for the light."
**the PI's version (produced in ~30s, in session):** "Deep night gave
me deep eyes, but I use it to seek the light."

## Per-row allocation (the exhibit's point)
| row | source carries | canonical | An |
|---|---|---|---|
| written-black (黑/黑) | doubled surface-black | partial: dark/black — two DIFFERENT dark words; print survives, doubling weakens | traded away: "deep" carries no black print |
| sound-repetition | 黑夜/黑色 lexical doubling | weakened (dark ≠ black) | RESTORED as true doubling: deep/deep |
| rhetoric-contrast (黑 ↔ 光明) | dark-to-light pivot | kept ("light") | kept ("light") |

Neither translation is globally better: each ALLOCATES the source's
traits differently — the canonical spends the doubling to keep the
black print; the PI's spends the black print to rebuild the doubling on
a different axis. Only per-trait scoring can state what was spent
where; holistic judgment cannot. This is the rubric's thesis in one
line, produced accidentally as a demonstration.

Status: pilot-board candidate (beside sonnet 73); the PI's version is a
reader-translator datum, n=1 by design. Filed on the vigil, #53.
