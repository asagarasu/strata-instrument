# Das Haus am Bache — Hans Heilmann (1905), attr. "Mei-Sheng" [青青河畔草]
**Provenance:** SBB Berlin digitized copy PPN1831265338 (PD mark), canvas 0071 = printed p.11, IIIF page image, transcribed by #52, 2026-07-20 night (Fraktur, legible). *Chinesische Lyrik vom 12. Jahrhundert v. Chr. bis zur Gegenwart* (Die Fruchtschale Bd. 1, Piper, München 1905). PD dual (d. 1930 EU + 1905 US). **This is Bethge's own prose crib** (his Geleitwort names it) — qingqing-de seat #2, and the PROSE stage of the de chain.

**FINDING (S8 exhibit; chain RESOLVED #53, 2026-07-19/20 — replaces
the #52 block that ordered the Gautier/Hervey check):** the moralized
close is **not Heilmann's either — it is GILES'S (1898), and Heilmann
is a line-for-line prose crib of Giles, 10/10 including the
"Mei-Sheng" attribution.** The Gautier/Hervey check ran NEGATIVE and
total: neither Hervey 1862 (Tang-only body; intro credits Li Ling/Su
Wu for five-word origins, no Mei Sheng, full text searched) nor
Gautier 1867/1902 (both TOCs print-verified) ever carried this poem —
the French line is not in this chain at all. The voice-theft
(空床難獨守 → warning to the husband; 蕩子行不歸 deleted) happened in
ENGLISH, in verse, rhyme-driven: the bird is the rhyme-fellow of "his
own" (own/flown). Heilmann's "sein Eigentum" is that English rhyme-tag
semanticized into German content; Bethge's "Kleinod" moralizes it
further. Full anatomy, witnesses, and the Giles text:
`giles_en_1898.md` in this directory. Also noted: 素手-line rendered
as "Ihr rundlicher Arm ist blendend weiß" (blinding white — an
illumination datum in the crib, = Giles's "dazzling white");
"Die Rosen ihrer Wangen glühn" adds rose/glow color absent from the
source line's 娥娥红粉妆 position (= Giles's "roses on her cheek blush
bright" — both color data enter via Giles too).

Grüner Rasen wächst am Ufer.
Die Weidenschößlinge stehn lang und dünn.
Eine Dame in schimmerndem Gewande
Öffnet das Fenster und blickt hernieder.
Die Rosen ihrer Wangen glühn,
Ihr rundlicher Arm ist blendend weiß;
Eine Sängerin ehemals,
Jetzt das muntere Weib eines Lebemannes.
Aber wenn er nicht gut auf sein Eigentum achtet,
Ist ihm eines Tages der Vogel entwischt!
