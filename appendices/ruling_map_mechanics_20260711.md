**Ruling 1 — schema consolidation mechanics (the PI, 07-11).** Freehand
human distillation of the schema is out ("a single human marking is as
loose as it can be"). A mechanical clustering proposer generates schema
candidates from the human marks; humans RATIFY (yes / no / unsure per
proposal). Nothing auto-merges. The proposer's similarity backend must
be fixed and non-generative (per the 07-10 ruling), its fingerprint
stamped on every output.

**Ruling 2 — grain doctrine (the PI, 07-11).** The canonical scheme is
**coarse field + value path of depth ≤ 2** (e.g. `living_thing,
plant_flower`). Hyponym collisions between markers normalize to
same-field/different-depth rather than being scored as disagreement.
Agreement is computable at both grains; depth is a registered
parameter.

**Ruling 3 — substitution (the PI, 07-11).** There is no authored
substitution rule ("should not be a sentence someone comes up with at
all"). Substitution = mechanical application of the ratified map:
ratified lines substitute everywhere they apply; unratified proposals
substitute nowhere.

**Ruling 4 — ratification protocol (recorded 07-11).** Two independent
ratifiers (A; C invited), sealed from each other's verdicts until both
land; time-boxed; pre-committed fallback = A's single ratification
stands if C misses the box. If both land, inter-ratifier agreement is
computed and reported; reconciliation is logged. The chair never
ratifies (proposer's author; #29 self-endorsement rule).

**EVOLUTION — 07-12 night, supersedes Ruling 1's ratification form
(added by #47 at sign-off, 07-13, so this appendix doesn't enshrine
a superseded regime).** Her method ruling, 07-12: "My rulings should
not be any law. We should fix algo instead of manual intervention."
Per-proposal human ratification (yes/no/unsure rows) is OUT; the
standing regime — the one v3.4 actually ran and was judged under —
is: **mechanism proposes with a-priori declared parameters; humans
judge WHOLESALE (usable / not usable + complaints); complaints are
falsification input that convicts and repairs the ALGORITHM; no
human verdict ever becomes a map line by hand.** Rulings 2 and 3
stand unchanged. Ruling 4 survives with "ratifier" read as "judge"
(two independent, blind, time-boxed, pre-committed fallback, chair
never judges). Round-1 ratification artifacts remain historical
record under the old regime, sealed as before.

**Provenance (chair's addendum, hygiene):** every applied map line
traces to a proposal ID and a mechanical rule (evidence star /
notation fold) with proposer class preserved per line; under the
07-12 regime the "named human verdict" column records the WHOLESALE
acceptance event, not per-line human acts — schema authority is
reported in the paper as: data, or grammar, never taste.
